# Optional AI intro shots (Runway Gen-4 / Google Veo / Flow)

The demo video is a real screen recording, which is what judges score. An AI clip can
replace the 6-second title card if you want a cinematic open. Generate 16:9, 5–8 s, no text
in the shot (add the title in the edit so it stays sharp).

**1. The ledger (recommended)**
Slow dolly-in on a dark control room desk at night. A single monitor shows a scrolling list of
refund approvals, green rows turning orange then red one by one. Soft orange rim light, shallow
depth of field, reflections on glass, quiet and tense, photorealistic, 35mm film look.

**2. The rewritten rulebook**
Macro shot of a printed rulebook page on a dark desk. The typed lines slowly rewrite themselves,
letters dissolving and reforming into different words, while ink bleeds orange at the edges.
Moody low-key lighting, slow push-in, photorealistic, cinematic.

**3. The delivery city**
Aerial night shot of Amsterdam canals, delivery riders with orange backpacks weaving over bridges.
Tilt up to a glowing data center on the skyline. Cinematic, moody blue and orange grade, slow motion.

To swap it in: replace `card_title.png` in the edit with the clip (6 s) and re-run the render.
